Mock 01 | Speaking examiner
PART 1 | 3-4 minutes
Good morning/afternoon. My name is ... . What is your name? How old are you? Where do you live? Ask both candidates.

Topic 1: home
Candidate A: Who do you live with? / Which room do you use most?
Candidate B: What do you like doing at home? / Is your home near your school?
Longer turn, Candidate A: Please tell me something about your favourite room.
Use a short prompt if needed, but do not supply an answer.

Topic 2: daily travel
Candidate A: How do you get to school? / Who do you travel with?
Candidate B: How long does the journey take? / Do you enjoy walking?
Longer turn, Candidate B: Please tell me something about a journey you often make.
Use a short prompt if needed, but do not supply an answer.

PART 2 | 5-6 minutes
Show the candidate card for Mock 01.
These pictures show free-time activities. Do you like these different things? Say why or why not. Talk together.
Allow the pair to exchange ideas for about 1-2 minutes. If necessary, ask about an unused picture: What about playing music?
Then invite each candidate to say which they prefer and why. Ask follow-up questions about whether one option is fun, useful, easy, expensive or interesting, choosing words that fit the activity.
Phase 2 questions to both candidates:
Do you prefer spending free time alone or with other people? Why?
Is it important to try new hobbies? Why?
Thank you. That is the end of the test.

Illustrative exchange, not a script for candidates to memorise:
Candidate A: I enjoy reading comics because it helps me relax. What do you think?
Candidate B: I prefer playing chess. I can do it with friends.
Candidate A: That sounds good. Do you do it often?
Candidate B: Sometimes at weekends. How about you?
Accept different opinions and reasons. Assess language and interaction rather than agreement.
