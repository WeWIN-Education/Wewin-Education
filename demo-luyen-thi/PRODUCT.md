# WeWIN Practice

## Register

product

## Users

Children practising English at Starters, Movers and Flyers levels, with parents registering and teachers reviewing their progress. Practice happens on both phones and computers, often alongside a parent at home.

## Product Purpose

Help children complete picture-led Reading and Listening practice, receive objective results, and obtain separate formative Writing feedback. The available catalog contains 60 tests across Starters 06–25, Movers 06–25 and Flyers 06–25, including an internally marked Hard range for each level.

## Brand Personality

Friendly, clear and encouraging. The established WeWIN blue, gold, logo and Win mascot belong to the approved visual direction A.

## Anti-references

The rejected direction B, intersecting decorative connectors, badges covering clothing, leaked listening scripts, neighbouring objects visible inside answer illustrations, and technical interaction boxes over characters.

## Design Principles

1. Make the learning task and picture details easy to understand.
2. Keep listening answers discoverable through audio, without text hints.
3. Use the approved Cambridge dashboard redesign: Overview, Practice, Results and Leaderboard. No XP, streaks or daily missions for occasional mock-test use.
4. Keep parent and staff explanations Vietnamese and the exam experience English.
5. Reduce image payload without losing text, objects or target alignment.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support tap and drag, keyboard navigation with a visible focus indicator, mobile layouts, light/dark themes and reduced motion. Keep text legible and never rely on colour alone to convey a result.
